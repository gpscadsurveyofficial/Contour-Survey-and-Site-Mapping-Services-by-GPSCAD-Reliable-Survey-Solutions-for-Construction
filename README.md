<h3>What Is a Contour Survey?</h3>
A contour survey measures ground elevations and represents terrain variations through contour lines on a map. These lines connect points of equal elevation, allowing engineers to understand the shape and slope of the land.<br>
Contour surveys are important for residential developments, commercial buildings, highways, industrial facilities, and large infrastructure projects. They help identify uneven terrain, evaluate drainage patterns, estimate earthwork requirements, and support effective site development.<br>
GPSCAD uses suitable surveying methods to collect elevation data and prepare contour information according to project requirements.<br>
<h3>Importance of Site Mapping</h3>
Site mapping provides a detailed picture of existing land conditions before development begins. Depending on the project scope, maps may include boundaries, ground levels, roads, structures, slopes, drainage features, and other physical elements.<br>
Professional mapping helps engineers and architects plan building locations, coordinate infrastructure layouts, and identify potential site constraints. Accurate information at the planning stage can also reduce avoidable design changes and improve construction efficiency.<br>
GPSCAD combines field measurements with digital processing to produce useful survey data for engineering and development applications.<br>
